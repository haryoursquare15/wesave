## 🎨 Visual Direction (all scripts)

- **Style:** 2D flat cartoon with subtle 3D depth (think *Cash App* meets *Duolingo*).
- **Palette:** Vestara Orange `#F5A623`, Gold `#E4B429`, Deep Navy `#0F1B2D`, Cream `#FFF6E6`.
- **Typography:** Bold sans (Satoshi / Cabinet Grotesk) for on-screen text. Numbers extra-bold.
- **Logo:** Use transparent PNG logo, bottom-right corner watermark from 0s, full lockup only at end card.
- **Motion:** Snappy 12fps character animation, 60fps camera/UI moves. Ease-out on every element entry.

## 🎙️ Voiceover Delivery Notes

- Tone: **warm, confident, slightly playful** — like a smart older sister.
- Accent: Neutral Nigerian English (not overly British, not fake American).
- Pauses at each product name. Emphasise numbers (**18%**, **daily**, **free**).
- Breath between scenes; do not rush the CTA.

## 🎵 Music

- Bed: Afro-pop instrumental, 110–120 BPM, no vocals.
- Suggested royalty-free: Epidemic Sound — *"Lagos Nights"*, *"Naija Bounce"*, *"Owambe Lite"*.
- Duck music -6dB under VO.

## 📦 Production Handoff

1. Pick script (recommend **Script 2** for launch week — clearest product showcase).
2. Record VO first (30s clean take + alt read).
3. Animate to VO timing in After Effects / CapCut / Canva Pro.
4. Export: 1080×1920 MP4, H.264, 30fps, ≤ 25MB, captions burned in (85% of IG plays muted).
5. Deliver 3 cutdowns: 30s (Reels/TikTok), 15s (Stories), 6s (YouTube bumper).
